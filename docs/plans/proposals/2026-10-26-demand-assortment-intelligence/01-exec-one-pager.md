# Mizan pilot: one-page summary

Date: 2026-10-26. Full proposal: [00-proposal.md](00-proposal.md). Money in USD; "k" is thousands.
Mizan is a working name.

## The ask

Fund a **USD 84k pilot** (Phases 0 and 1, 9 Nov 2026 to 30 Apr 2027) [04]. It runs on U.S. Polo Assn. (USPA),
Cardio Bunny and one hardline brand through Ramadan and Eid 2027. A holdout test measures the real lift: half the
products follow Mizan and a matched half keep today's method.

The pilot is an extension of Channel Replenishment, as DD10 recommended. Leadership decides on the full product
at Gate 1 (May 2027), and only on measured results.

## Why now

- Stock is planned from the last 14 to 60 days of sales, which misses moving events [06]. White Friday 2025
  lifted daily revenue +50% to +64%. Ramadan 2025 lifted USPA about +50% [03]. Ramadan 2027 starts about
  8 Feb [02].
- Stock is short and long at once:
  - 48% of selling softline SKU-locations have under 14 days of cover, and 19% have over 120 days.
  - Up to 41% of stock value is over 180 days old.
  - Up to USD 6.7k a day of softline demand sits on empty shelves [03].
- 15 of 18 brands started selling on or after 29 Oct 2025, so every forecast is a "new brand" forecast [03].
- NPD has no competitor data. opptra.com promises "Revenue and volume forecasting" with no forecast behind it
  [DD10].
- A senior data scientist must be in place by 21 Dec 2026, or the pilot misses Ramadan 2027 [05].

## The numbers

| Full product (USD 0.68M over 3 years) | Low | Base | High |
|---|---|---|---|
| Annual benefit at full adoption | 54k | 184k | 525k |
| 3-year NPV (at 12%) | -638k | **-296k** | +339k |
| One-time cash freed from lower stock | 191k | 353k | 486k |

- **At today's USD 13.4M revenue, the full product does not pay back in 3 years in the base case.** It needs
  USD 376k a year of benefit, about twice the base estimate [04].
- The base case breaks even within 3 years at about **USD 29M of gross revenue a year**, 2.15 times the last
  12 months [calc]. A full year's benefit covers a full year's running cost from about USD 20M. Time to USD 29M,
  by growth in units [calc]:
  - 10% a year (the model's cap): about 8 years; 5-year NPV -281k.
  - 30% a year: about 3 years; 5-year NPV +20k.
  - 50% a year: about 2 years; 5-year NPV +441k.
- Actual unit growth is much faster: GCC units +49% in the last 6 months against the 6 before, and the run rate
  is already 1.2 times the last 12 months [03], [calc]. But it comes from new brands and channels. Like-for-like
  USPA units are flat (-1%) [03]. So the full product depends on the launch pipeline; the pilot does not.
- **Stage-gating caps the risk at USD 84k.** Expected NPV is -185k gated against -437k if all 3 years were funded
  up front [04].
- **The lean alternative:** "Mizan Lite" (event uplift inside Replenishment plus a one-off competitor scrape)
  costs 138k over 3 years, with a base NPV of about -29k [05], [calc]. The pilot builds it anyway, so stopping at
  Gate 1 still leaves Opptra with Mizan Lite, at about 34k a year to run.
- **Not in the ROI:**
  - coverage of every brand, including hardline (34% of the modelled benefit)
  - a provable partner pitch
  - NPD evidence for Cardio Bunny against BlissClub and Lululemon
