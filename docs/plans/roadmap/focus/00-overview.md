# Focus 00 - Overview: the 7 initiatives the business chose (read this first)

> **Bottom line.** **What:** 7 ways to fill missing product photos: 2 by cropping (no AI), 5 by AI generation.
> **Why:** base revenue of 32.5k USD a year for the crops and 75.6k for the five generated items, all judged.
> **Blocks it:** owner decisions first (cropping amendment, photo roles), then the generation package and brand rights.
> **Sign:** the sheet in section 5, one yes / no per line. Start with crops; send every outside ask now (free).

Date: 2026-10-26. Documents only; nothing is built and no paid call was run. No new estimate: every figure is copied
with a pointer. No time or effort figures (owner rule, 08 L7-9). Short codes: **08** =
`roadmap/08-reprioritization-proposal.md`, **F01** to **F04** = the four focus docs in this folder.

## 1. What this folder is

Interim approval pages for the 7 initiatives the business picked as an immediate need. The original roadmap docs
00 to 08 are unchanged. **08 is the source of truth**; F01 to F04 copy from it and from the docs it cites.

**Words used (defined once).** **Crop:** a new photo cut from a larger brand photo of the same product, no pixel
changed. **Generate:** an image generator (a paid AI service that draws images) makes a new photo from the brand's
own photos. **Composite:** the brand's real cut-out product pasted by code onto a generated background, so product
pixels stay identical. **SPEC amendment:** an owner-approved change to `docs/SPEC.md`, the rule book of what the
catalog module may do; today it forbids cropping, generation and lifestyle backgrounds (08 L24-26). **Gate:**
automatic checks plus a person's review that a generated image did not change the product (08 L27-28).
**Base:** the best judged case of extra revenue in USD a year; an estimate, not a measurement (08 L19-20).
**Holdout:** fix half of a matched set of products, leave the other half, compare sales (08 L29). **Golden set:**
photos a person labels by hand so the role checks can be scored. **Wave:** 08's grouping by what unblocks an item
(B = owner decisions, C = outside answers, D = generator and gate). **DD2, I5, I06:** deep dive 2, initiatives 05 and
06 in `docs/plans/`; "R4" or "Q7" is a recommendation or question number inside them.

## 3. Recommended order and why

1. **Crops first (5.1a, then 5.1b).** No generator, no gate, no brand rights; only owner decisions plus, for 5.1b,
   outside answers (08 L56-63, L67-69). 5.1a is "the largest revenue item that your own decisions can unlock"
   (08 L69). 5.1b needs 5.1a done, plus channel specs and larger masters (08 L315).
2. **Then the generation package (decision 2) unlocks the other five at once** (08 L101). Inside it, 08 orders by
   value: 6.1b, 5.2, 5.3, 6.2, 6.1a (Wave D rows D1 to D5, 08 L454-460). Two fixed rules: 5.3 needs all of 5.2
   (08 L317); and the gate that lifestyle reuses is built and labelled under 5.2 (F03 blocker 7; I06 L298).
3. **6.1a vs 6.1b rule:** 6.1a is not "after" 6.1b; it is the same build with another brand's written yes.
   Whichever brand confirms first goes first. If Campus or Keds confirms before US Polo, 6.1a runs first, because its
   reviewed images become the 100 labels the gate needs before 6.1b can leave full review (08 L355-357, L460; F03 s7).
4. **6.2** shares the lifestyle build and is also its fallback for kids and failed images (08 L344; F04 s7).
5. **Free now:** send every outside ask (brand rights, vendor position, channel specs, masters) (08 L70-71, L145).
